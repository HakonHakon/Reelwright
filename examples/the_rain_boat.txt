THE RAIN BOAT

EXT. BACKYARD - MORNING, JUST AFTER RAIN

The grass sparkles. Puddles shine like little mirrors. Drops fall from the fence and the apple tree.

The back door creaks open. SAM, a small boy in his teal raincoat with little yellow trees, steps out in red rubber boots. He looks at the biggest puddle, then jumps in with both feet. Splash! He giggles.

Something white floats on the water: a tiny paper boat, rocking gently.

SAM
Boat!

Sam crouches at the edge of the puddle, cheeks round, and blows on the paper sail. The boat glides slowly across the water.

A red ladybug lands on the boat, right on the tip of the sail.

SAM
Hello, ladybug.

The clouds open. Warm sunlight spills across the yard, and a soft rainbow appears above the fence. Sam points up, eyes wide.

SAM
Look! Rainbow!

The ladybug opens its wings and flies off toward the rainbow. Sam stands up and waves with his whole arm.

SAM
Bye bye!

He looks down at his little boat, smiles, and gives it one more gentle push.

FADE OUT.
